**Description** (4000)

```
Clipeo Scoreboard is a volleyball scoreboard built for use on court: the numbers fill the whole screen and everything works with one finger.

TAP TO SCORE, SWIPE TO CORRECT
Tap a team's half to give them a point and swipe down to take it back. No tiny buttons or menus in the middle of a rally.

SETS
Each team has its own set counter with the same gestures. Turn on automatic set end (to 25, 21, 15 or any target, with or without a 2-point lead) and match end at the best of the sets you choose, with a deciding set.

SCORING MODES
Create the variations you use in practice:
• Double point: one rally worth 2.
• 3 in a row = 1: win several rallies to score a single point.
• Any combination of rallies needed and points awarded, in a row or cumulative.
Switch modes with one tap and see each team's progress under the score.

YOUR GESTURES
Choose what each gesture does: tap, long press, swipe down or swipe up. For example, long press for a double point and swipe up to undo.

UNDO
Made a mistake? Undo the last change, whether it was a point, a set or a side switch.

YOUR STYLE
• Team names and colors, any color you like.
• Switch sides with one tap.
• Landscape or portrait, on phones and tablets.
• Light and dark mode, and the screen stays on during the match.

NO ACCOUNTS, NO ADS
No sign-up. No ads or tracking: the score and your settings are stored only on your device.

Clipeo Scoreboard is part of Clipeo (clipeostats.com).
```
